# ecoscan-ai-sdg12
Artificial Intelligence Final Project SubmissionProject Title: EcoScan AI: Smart Computer Vision Waste Sorting & Carbon Impact CalculatorTarget SDGs: Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) & Goal 13 (Climate Action)1. Problem StatementRapid urbanization and consumer waste have led to critical ecological pressures globally. According to United Nations estimates, over 2 billion tons of municipal solid waste are generated annually, with less than 20% being recycled properly. A primary driver of low recycling rates is sorting confusion: consumers frequently struggle to distinguish recyclable materials, compostables, hazardous waste, and non-recyclable trash at the point of disposal. This leads to high contamination rates at recycling processing facilities, forcing entire batches of recyclable material into landfills and accelerating global greenhouse gas emissions.2. Project ObjectiveThe primary objective of EcoScan AI is to empower individuals and local communities to make accurate, instant waste disposal decisions using AI-driven visual recognition. The application aims to:Automatically classify waste items (plastics, paper/cardboard, glass, organic waste, e-waste, and landfill trash) using camera streams or uploaded images.Provide localized, step-by-step disposal instructions and bin color indicators (Recycle, Compost, Trash, Hazardous).Quantify the real-time carbon footprint offset ($CO_2e$) saved per correctly recycled item to incentivize sustainable habits and positive behavioral change.3.
